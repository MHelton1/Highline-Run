Highline Run

Build 39. New: every car has a performance rating and class (D to X), and road races are limited to a class, so take parts off or pick a slower car for them (refitting parts is free). Menu switches for crash damage (garages repair it), easier speedway rivals, and interstate extras: traffic, top speed traps, Mile 10 Truck Stop and drag races. The hypercar has its own body. No traffic on the oval, and smoother speedway ground: the pit lane meets the track without a drop, and the Sundown Flats roundabout is level. Build 38: clear every goal on all three maps to win the Zenith 300 hypercar and open a bonus map, Ironhorse Speedway: a banked oval with a pit lane, a tunnel out to the town of Trackside, and a 20 mile interstate to Sundown Flats, where a classic endurance racer is hidden. Build 37: stronger brakes (60 to 0 mph in about 22 m), the car slows when you lift off, and quicker steering. Build 36: drive under bridges, guardrails, buildings and posts stop the whole car, smoother river banks, and auto throttle to 80 mph (menu): press the throttle to go faster, the brake to take over. Build 35: driving tests (menu, Driving tests): five short tests per map in a yellow school car, with medals that pay once. Build 34: everyday traffic on the ring road and outer loop, slowing through towns (switch it off in the menu). Build 33: a long Grand Prix round each outer loop, two new goals per map (win it, and drive through all three towns), and the coast's island road no longer blocked where a side road joins. Build 32: every map is half as wide again, with an outer loop road, three side roads, three new pickup spots and a third town out on the loop (on the coast it hops between islands on long bridges). Build 31: two towns on every map, around the garages, with lit windows at night. Build 30: two rivers on Saltcliff Coast, with the coast road crossing them on bridges. Build 29: Open map for two players, kid mode in two-player races, front-wheel dirt and tyre tracks on dirt, smoother movement, harder time trial gold, full screen and Exit. Build 28: change any key or controller button in the menu, Controls, with button names for Xbox, Switch and PlayStation controllers. Build 27: two players on one screen (menu, Two players) with a keyboard or two controllers. Build 26: in rain the wheels throw mud off dirt and spray off wet roads; in snow, white powder. Tips come at most once a minute. Build 25 added a daily challenge (see the menu), tyre marks and nitro flames. Recent: tips for new players, a nitro kit at the garages, drift scoring and speed traps, and a big map: tap the corner map to drop a marker. Three regions to explore: the Highline Mountains, the Redrock Basin desert and the Saltcliff Coast. Run cargo past the patrols, buy and tune cars at the garages, race six events and a three-race cup in each region, hunt for hidden cars and crates, and work through the goals. Weather and time of day change as you drive. Kid mode makes the car drive itself.
