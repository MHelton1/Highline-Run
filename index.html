Build 30. New: two rivers on Saltcliff Coast, with the coast road crossing them on bridges. Build 29: Open map for two players, kid mode in two-player races, front-wheel dirt and tyre tracks on dirt, smoother movement, harder time trial gold, full screen and Exit. Tips come at most once a minute. Build 25 added a daily challenge (see the menu), tyre marks and nitro flames. Recent: tips for new players, a nitro kit at the garages, drift scoring and speed traps, and a big map: tap the corner map to drop a marker. Three regions to explore: the Highline Mountains, the Redrock Basin desert and the Saltcliff Coast.
